# Track data

Circuit centerlines and track widths are taken from
[TUMFTM/racetrack-database](https://github.com/TUMFTM/racetrack-database)
(Technical University of Munich, Institute of Automotive Technology),
licensed under LGPL-3.0. Files are unmodified; re-download with
`python scripts/fetch_tracks.py`.

Columns: `x_m, y_m, w_tr_right_m, w_tr_left_m`
